Privacy Policy for The Zombies Won't stop

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JoyCode Studios ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you play The Zombies Won't Stop on Meta Quest devices.

1. Information We Collect Directly

We do not directly collect, store, or sell your personal information (such as your name, email address, or physical location).

Game progress, settings, weapon unlocks, and high scores are saved locally on your headset and are never transmitted to our servers.

2. Third-Party Services & Automatic Diagnostics

Our game is built using the Unity game engine (v2021.3.45f2) and distributed via the Meta Horizon Store. By default, these third-party platforms may automatically collect limited technical data to keep the game running and diagnose crashes, including:

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Engine): Collects basic hardware specifications, operating system version, device identifiers, IP address, and crash/diagnostic logs under its default engine telemetry. You can read more in the Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (Meta Horizon OS): Processes standard platform and device telemetry required to run apps on Meta Quest hardware. You can read more in the Meta Privacy Policy.

3. How This Data Is Used

Any technical or diagnostic data collected by Unity or Meta is used solely to ensure hardware compatibility, measure general performance, and fix software crashes or bugs.

4. Data Retention & Your Deletion Rights (UK GDPR)

Because we do not operate external servers or store your personal data ourselves, we hold no personal records to delete. Local save files can be removed at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app data on your Meta Quest headset.

If you wish to request the deletion of any diagnostic or installation identifiers processed by Unity or Meta, or if you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at macaulayfernandeschannel@gmail.com and we will assist with your request.
